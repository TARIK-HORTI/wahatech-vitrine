How we verify each cooperative

Before a cooperative appears on WahaTech, our team checks its file.

  1. 1

    An official file

    Certificate of registration in the cooperatives register and the identity of its representative.

  2. 2

    Checked documents

    The ONSSA health authorisation and labels (organic, PGI) are only displayed if provided and valid.

  3. 3

    A signed commitment

    Contract, quality charter and complaint follow-up: a cooperative that breaks its commitments is suspended.
