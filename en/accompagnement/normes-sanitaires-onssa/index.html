Health standards and ONSSA authorisation

Mandatory to sell food products: upgrading the unit, good hygiene practices and the health authorisation or approval file.

  • Diagnosis of the production unit and upgrade plan
  • Hygiene, cleaning and batch traceability procedures
  • Preparing the health authorisation or approval file
  • Preparing for the inspection visit
  • Compliant labelling: mandatory information, batch, dates
