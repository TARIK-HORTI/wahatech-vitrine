General terms of use

Provisional document. The final version will be written with a legal professional before WahaTech opens to the public.

Disclaimer

WahaTech is solely an advertising and technical intermediary. WahaTech sells no products, collects no payment for products and makes no deliveries. Each cooperative is solely responsible, legally, in health matters, commercially and for tax purposes, for the products it presents and sells.

1. Operator and purpose

The wahatech.ma website (“WahaTech”) is operated by a private individual, who can be reached at contact@wahatech.ma.

WahaTech offers: a display space (showcase) for subscribed agricultural cooperatives; forwarding visitors' order requests to cooperatives; brokering for bulk orders and export; on-demand digital and consulting services for cooperatives. These terms apply to anyone using the site.

2. WahaTech's role

WahaTech is not a seller, reseller, depositary or carrier. WahaTech does not buy, store or price the products and is not a party to the sales contracts concluded between the cooperatives and their customers.

Displayed prices are set by the cooperatives; they are indicative, and the final price, delivery costs and payment terms are confirmed by the cooperative before any sale.

3. Responsibility of the cooperatives

Each cooperative guarantees the accuracy of the information it publishes and alone bears, towards customers and the authorities:

  • the price, invoicing and collection of its sales;
  • the quality, compliance and safety of its products, including labelling;
  • holding valid health authorisations (ONSSA) and quality signs (organic, PGI, designation of origin, agricultural label);
  • the preparation, shipping and delivery of products, as well as returns and complaints;
  • compliance with Law 31-08 on consumer protection and with its tax and social obligations.

WahaTech checks the documents submitted at registration (registration number and, if provided, the ONSSA authorisation number); this document check is neither a product inspection nor a guarantee of quality. Displaying an ONSSA number is optional on WahaTech and does not exempt the cooperative from its health obligations.

4. Order requests

In the shop, the visitor prepares a request and gives their contact details. WahaTech sends a request to each cooperative concerned, by email and in its space, with these details. The cooperative contacts the customer, confirms or declines the request, delivers and collects payment itself. The request is not a firm order until the cooperative has confirmed it with the customer.

WahaTech neither requests nor receives any payment for products. Any complaint about a product, price, delivery or payment must be addressed to the cooperative; WahaTech may, simply as a courtesy, help reach the cooperative.

5. Bulk orders and export

Above the threshold shown on the site, or through the “Quote request” form, the request goes to the WahaTech team, which consults one or more cooperatives or groups and passes on their offer. WahaTech then acts as a broker: the sales contract is concluded directly between the buyer and the selected producer, and WahaTech's commission is owed by the producer under the contract signed with them.

6. Cooperative subscription

A cooperative's space is an annual advertising service, governed by a service agreement signed with the operator. The subscription is paid by bank transfer; a service receipt is issued on activation. Without an active subscription, the cooperative's page and products are no longer displayed. On-demand digital and consulting services are quoted separately.

7. Content and conduct

Each member undertakes to provide accurate information, to respect other members and to publish only lawful content to which they hold the rights (texts, photos, logos). WahaTech may remove content or suspend an account that breaks these rules.

8. Limitation of liability

WahaTech provides the site as is and strives to keep it available, without guaranteeing uninterrupted service. To the extent permitted by law, WahaTech cannot be held liable for products, sales, deliveries, payments or disputes between a cooperative and its customers, nor for content published by members.

9. Personal data

Details entered in a request are passed to the cooperative concerned (or, for a bulk order, to the WahaTech team) for the sole purpose of handling that request. Data is processed in accordance with Law 09-08; details are in the privacy policy. Privacy policy

10. Governing law

These terms are governed by Moroccan law. WahaTech may amend them; the version in force is the one published on this page.
