Recommendations medium priority

Moderate water stress detected

  • Water more often: +20 to 30% more water, preferably early in the morning or in the evening.
  • Check the irrigation network (clogged drippers, pressure, leaks, state of the channels).
  • Mulch the soil (crop residues, straw) to limit evaporation.
  • Check the plot every 2 to 3 days.
  • Date palm: irrigate in a basin at the foot, especially during fruit set and date sizing (April–September).
