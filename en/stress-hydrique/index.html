HydroStress Beta

Water stress of your plots

Draw your plot on the satellite map: WahaTech combines Sentinel-2 images with rain and temperature over the last 30 days to estimate whether your crops lack water, with advice suited to your region.

Beta version. During the test phase, analyses use demonstration (simulated) data to show how it works. Real satellite analysis will open at the official launch.
Click on the map to place the corners of the plot.
