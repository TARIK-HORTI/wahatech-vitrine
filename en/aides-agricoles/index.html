Farm subsidies guide

Agricultural Development Fund (FDA) subsidies, then loans, guarantees and support programmes for businesses, cooperatives and rural projects, explained simply.

This guide summarises public information. Rates, ceilings and crop lists change: always check with the DPA or ORMVA in your area before investing. FDA amounts are copied from the Ministry's official guide, 2026 edition.
Irrigation Ministry of Agriculture (FDA)

Localised irrigation (drip)

Well or borehole, pumping, head station, pipes, drippers, automation and storage basin. Order 1323.22, valid until 31/12/2027 or until 350,000 ha are approved. Amounts unchanged in 2026.

Rate and ceiling :
Collective projects: 100%, ceiling 38,000 DH per net equipped ha (+ basin 50 DH/m³, max 12,000 DH/ha).
Small farmers: 100%, ceiling 38,000 DH/ha (+ basin max 12,000 DH/ha).
Farms over 5 up to 20 ha: 75%, ceiling 28,500 DH/ha (+ basin max 9,000 DH/ha).
Over 20 ha: 60%, ceiling 23,000 DH/ha (+ basin max 7,000 DH/ha).

How to apply

1) Prior approval BEFORE works; 2) works; 3) subsidy request with original detailed invoices, proof of payment and bank details, within 24 months (30 for collective projects). Water authorisation and drilling authorisation required. Lease: at least 6 years remaining. Equipment to standard NM 12.1.100. File at the one-stop desk (guichet unique) of the DPA or ORMVA for your area, against a dated, signed receipt. Paid by bank transfer.

Irrigation Ministry of Agriculture (FDA)

Supplementary irrigation (sprinklers)

Wells, pumping, sprinklers and basin to supplement rainfall (order 1323.22).

Rate and ceiling :
Collective projects: 100%, ceiling 34,000 DH/ha (+ basin max 10,000 DH/ha).
Farms up to 20 ha: 75%, ceiling 25,000 DH/ha (+ basin 35 DH/m³, max 8,000 DH/ha).
Over 20 ha: 60%, ceiling 20,000 DH/ha (+ basin 25 DH/m³, max 6,000 DH/ha).

How to apply

Same steps as drip irrigation: prior approval before works, then the subsidy request within 24 months (30 for collective projects). File at the one-stop desk (guichet unique) of the DPA or ORMVA for your area, against a dated, signed receipt. Paid by bank transfer.

Irrigation Ministry of Agriculture (FDA)

Solar pumping for irrigation

Programme (agreement of 19/02/2024, 51,000 ha targeted) to replace butane pumping with a solar kit: panels, controller, pump, structure, water meter. One subsidy per farm.

Rate and ceiling :
30% of kit cost, ceilings: 3,000 DH per irrigated ha, 3,000 DH per installed kWp, 30,000 DH per project.

How to apply

A working drip system, current butane pumping and a water meter with a log are required. Qualified installer (certificate 3-1 or 9-1, or Taqa Pro label). Registration in the national farm register (RNA); if leased, at least 12 months remaining. Prior approval, then subsidy request within 3 months with paid invoices, warranties and a 5-year commitment. No direct payment to suppliers. File at the one-stop desk (guichet unique) of the DPA or ORMVA for your area, against a dated, signed receipt. Paid by bank transfer.

Planting and seeds Ministry of Agriculture (FDA)

Date palm planting

Densifying and rehabilitating palm groves, and extension with vitro-plants (order 3809.15).

Rate and ceiling :
Densification or rehabilitation: 100% of plant cost, given as plants.
Extension (at least 100 palms/ha): 70% of plant cost, ceiling 35,000 DH/ha.

How to apply

Extension: prior approval, then final vitro-plant invoices and a 5-year commitment, within 12 months. File at the one-stop desk (guichet unique) of the DPA or ORMVA for your area, against a dated, signed receipt. Paid by bank transfer.

Planting and seeds Ministry of Agriculture (FDA)

Olive, argan, fig and other trees

New plantations with certified plants from approved nurseries (orders 1877.13 and 2397.10).

Rate and ceiling :
Olive: 5,000 DH/ha on drip (at least 285 plants/ha); 3,500 DH/ha irrigated (100-284 plants/ha) or rainfed (at least 100 plants/ha).
Argan: 80% of plant cost, ceiling 6,000 DH/ha rainfed (100 plants/ha) or 9,000 DH/ha irrigated (200 plants/ha).
60% of plant cost, ceiling per ha: irrigated fig 6,000 (230 plants) or rainfed 3,500 (140 plants), pomegranate 6,000 (200 plants), walnut 10,000 (70 plants), carob 6,000 (100 plants), pistachio 8,000 (200 plants).

How to apply

Prior approval (form, ID, link to the land, project sheet), then the subsidy request within 12 months with "certified plants" invoices and a 5-year commitment. File at the one-stop desk (guichet unique) of the DPA or ORMVA for your area, against a dated, signed receipt. Paid by bank transfer.

Planting and seeds Ministry of Agriculture (FDA)

Replanting after fire blight or tristeza

For orchards uprooted because of disease, in areas designated by ministerial order.

Rate and ceiling :
Olive: 12,000 DH/ha.
Peach, apricot, plum and cherry: 15,000 DH/ha.
Citrus (tristeza): no amount in the guide's table, ask the DPA.
Cannot be combined with the new-planting aid for the same plot.

How to apply

ONSSA uprooting and burning certificate, certified plant invoices (citrus: rootstock other than sour orange), 5-year commitment. Request within 24 months of the uprooting certificate. File at the one-stop desk (guichet unique) of the DPA or ORMVA for your area, against a dated, signed receipt. Paid by bank transfer.

Planting and seeds Ministry of Agriculture (FDA)

Certified cereal and pulse seeds

The aid is deducted directly from the sale price at approved seed companies (order 2476.25, 2025-2026 season).

Rate and ceiling :
Seeds produced in Morocco, per quintal: soft wheat 255 DH, durum wheat 235 DH, barley 285 DH, oat and triticale 180 DH; faba bean 205 DH, lentil and chickpea 395 DH, forage pea and vetch 180 DH.
Imported cereal seeds, per quintal: pre-basic G3 800 DH, basic G4 500 DH, first generation R1 500 DH.

How to apply

Nothing to file: buy from an approved dealer, the discount is applied on the invoice. Seed companies also get a storage aid (5 DH per quintal per month, for 9 months).

Purebred dairy heifers

Buying imported heifers or purebred heifers bred in Morocco (order 1902.23). According to the 2026 guide, the aid ends on 30/09/2026, or earlier if the planned number of head is reached: ask the DPA whether it has been extended.

Rate and ceiling :
Imported heifer: 6,000 DH per head (up to 60,000 head).
Purebred heifer bred in Morocco: 3,000 DH per head (up to 120,000 head).

How to apply

CIAB card, certified pedigree and invoice. Request within 6 months of purchase (imported) or 2 months (national). File at the one-stop desk (guichet unique) of the DPA or ORMVA for your area, against a dated, signed receipt. Paid by bank transfer.

Selected breeding stock and camels

Producing selected sheep, goats and bull calves (order 2719.22, until 31/12/2026), and buying breeding camels (order 3380.15).

Rate and ceiling :
Sheep: 800 DH per male, 700 DH per female (850 / 750 DH for cooperatives or groups).

Equipment and mechanisation Ministry of Agriculture (FDA)

Tractors

Buying new tractors (order 1051.18). Number subsidised by area: 1 under 5 ha, 2 for 5-10 ha, 3 for 10-20 ha, 4 for 20-50 ha, 5 for 50-100 ha, then 1 per extra 100 ha.

Rate and ceiling :
30% of the price, ceiling by power (under 50 hp / 50 to 70 hp / 70 hp and more):
2WD: 52,000 / 62,000 / 72,000 DH.
4WD: 60,000 / 70,000 / 80,000 DH.
Aggregation project: +10% in the 2nd instalment (17,000 to 27,000 DH).

How to apply

Prior approval BEFORE buying or building, then the subsidy request with original invoices. Registration card or receipt, commitment to keep the machine 5 years; request within 12 months of approval. Farm service companies (run by an agricultural engineer or technician) can also apply: 1 tractor per 500 ha worked per year, 10 at most. File at the one-stop desk (guichet unique) of the DPA or ORMVA for your area, against a dated, signed receipt. Paid by bank transfer.

Trailed and PTO-driven tools, seed drills and planters (order 1051.18).

Damane loan guarantees

Open to Moroccan companies in all sectors. Guarantee rate depends on the product.

Loans, guarantees and microcredit Microcredit associations

Microcredit

Small loans with support for very small projects, self-employed people and small farm activities (Al Amana, Attawfiq, Ardi, FONDEP…).

Rate and ceiling :
Ceiling raised from 50,000 to 150,000 DH by law 50-20 (2021), depending on the project. Rates and terms vary by institution.

How to apply

Go directly to a branch in your area with your ID and a description of your activity.

Businesses and cooperatives Regional investment centres (CRI)

Investment grant for small and medium businesses

New grant under the Investment Charter (decree 2.25.342 of 12/06/2025). Priority sectors include agri-food processing and aquaculture.

Rate and ceiling :
Up to 30% of the investment, combining: stable jobs 5 to 10%, province 10 or 15% (15% e.g. in Zagora, Figuig, Tata), priority sector 10%.
Investment of 1 to 50 million DH.

How to apply

Existing company with turnover of 1 to 200 million DH, at least 10% own funds, at least one stable job per million invested. Apply online at CRI-invest.ma; reviewed by the regional unified investment commission.

Businesses and cooperatives Forsa programme (State)

Forsa: honour loan for project holders

Public programme for people with an idea or project: training, mentoring and an interest-free honour loan. Terms change from one edition to the next.

Rate and ceiling :
2022 edition: up to 100,000 DH interest-free, including a 10,000 DH grant, repayable over 10 years with a 2-year grace period. Check the amounts of the current edition.

How to apply

Register online during the call for applications, then selection, training and funding.
