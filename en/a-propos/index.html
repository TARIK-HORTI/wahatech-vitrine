About

From the cooperatives' land to your table

WahaTech presents the products of Moroccan cooperatives, passes customer requests on to them and supports them from creation to marketing.

Our mission

All over Morocco, cooperatives produce high-quality argan oil, honey, saffron, dates and amlou. Yet many struggle to sell beyond their region for lack of visibility, logistics or support.

Born in the oases of Tafilalet, WahaTech gives them an online store and supports them at every step, in Arabic, French and English.

The local products store

  • Straight from the cooperatives. Each product page shows the cooperative that made it, its region and its story.
  • No account needed. You send your request with your name, phone number and address. It goes to the cooperative, which calls you.
  • Directly with the cooperative. Price, delivery and payment are settled directly with it: WahaTech is an advertising and technical intermediary, not a seller.

Support for cooperatives

Setup, funding, compliance, branding, sales: a team that knows the field supports your cooperative at every stage, in your language.

Discover our support →

Our commitments

  • A fair price. The cooperative gets a fair price for its work.
  • Protected data. Your details are used only to pass your request on to the cooperative concerned. They are never published or sold.
  • Listening to members. Every suggestion and every complaint is read and helps improve the platform.
