Frequently asked questions

Rates, expert verification, data protection: answers to the most common questions about WahaTech.

For farmers

Is registration paid?

No. Registration, searching for experts and declaring your farm are free.

How much does a consultation cost?

Each expert sets their own rates and shows them on their profile as a guide. The final price is agreed directly between you and the expert.

How do I contact an expert?

Log in, open their profile and use their phone or WhatsApp if they made them visible.

Are the experts really qualified?

Each expert sends their diploma, CV or professional card. The team checks these documents before the profile appears in the directory.

Who sees my farm information?

Only you. With your consent, it is used anonymously and in aggregate for regional statistics. It is never published or sold.

For experts

Who can register as an expert?

Agronomists, agricultural technicians and agricultural consultants who can prove their training or experience.

Which documents should I send?

A diploma, a detailed CV, or a professional card or employment certificate. PDF, JPG or PNG, 5 MB maximum per document.

How long does verification take?

The team replies within 72 working hours. If a document is missing, you receive a message explaining what to add.

Are my documents published?

No. They are stored in a private space and read only by the WahaTech team.
