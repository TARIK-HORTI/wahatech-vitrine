Help

Frequently asked questions

Requests to cooperatives, delivery, payment, bulk orders and support: answers to the most frequently asked questions about WahaTech.

Sending a request in the shop

Does WahaTech sell the products?

No. WahaTech is an advertising and technical intermediary: the shop presents the cooperatives' products and passes your request on to them. The sale is between you and the cooperative, which sets the price, delivers and collects payment.

Do I need an account to send a request?

No. Just give your name, phone number and address. With an account, you will find your requests in “My requests”.

How does payment work?

You pay the cooperative directly, as agreed with it on the phone. WahaTech does not collect any payment for the products.

How much does delivery cost?

Each cooperative organises its own delivery and tells you its cost and lead time when it calls you to confirm your request.

How do I track my request?

Each request gets a reference (WT-…) and a tracking page. The cooperative sets its status itself: sent, confirmed, delivered or cancelled.

What about large quantities or export?

Above 200 kg, or with the “Quote request” form, your request goes to the WahaTech team, which consults one or more cooperatives and sends you an offer. The sales contract is still concluded directly with the selected cooperative or group.

Where do the products come from?

Directly from the cooperatives that make them. Each product page shows the cooperative, its region and its story.

Selling online (cooperatives)

How do we show our products in the shop?

Create a cooperative account and complete your profile, then turn on “shown in the shop” for your finished products, with a price and stock. Customer requests arrive by email and in your space; you confirm, deliver and collect payment yourselves.

How are customer requests handled?

You receive each request by email, with the customer's details, and in the “Requests received” tab of your space. You call the customer, confirm the price and delivery, deliver and collect payment directly, then update the status.

Support

What services do you offer?

Eleven services across four stages, from setting up the cooperative to selling, plus three packs that bundle them.

Do I need to have a cooperative already?

No. We also support projects: articles of association, founding general meeting and registration file.

How do I get started?

Send a request from the Support page. A WahaTech advisor will contact you within 2 working days for a free first conversation.
